How a Project Moves

1. Measure or Scan

Field dimensions, photos, or a Matterport / LiDAR scan. Send what you already have.

2. Draft

Floor plan, electrical, and the sheets a permit reviewer will look at. Typical drafting is 24–48 hours.

3. Revisions

A set number of revisions is included so the drawing matches what you intend to build.

4. Permit Submittal

PDF for the city, and CAD for contractors who keep drawing. Then the application goes in.

Frequently Asked Questions

What services do you offer?

Permit-ready floor plans and electrical plans, 3D renderings, as-builts, and 3D scanning. General contractors can put repeat plan sets on a monthly retainer. Bell Designs Utah also contracts the construction: Licensed & Insured, Utah B100 [LICENSE #].

How long does a project take?

Most drafting is finished 24–48 hours after measurements or a scan are in hand. Larger houses take longer, and you hear the timeline before work starts. Construction schedules depend on the scope. Retainer clients are booked first.

Do you provide revisions?

Yes. Drafting includes a set number of revisions so the sheets match the project. On contracted construction, change orders are allowed. Work past the included revisions may be an additional cost.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Licensed & Insured, Utah B100 [LICENSE #]. Chris Bell holds a Utah B100 general contractor license, and the business carries insurance.

Do you work with general contractors?

Yes. Builders send basement and remodel work for permit-ready sets, as-builts, and scans. Homeowners use the same drawings. Retainer packages are described on the services page.
