Files, Scans, and Turnaround

What You Send

Field measurements, a marked-up PDF, or phone photos. A Matterport or LiDAR link when the existing house should be scanned. The city, so the set follows that jurisdiction. Upload files on the contact form, or email belldesignsutah@gmail.com.

What You Get Back

A PDF plan set for permit submittal. CAD (DWG) for retainer clients who keep drawing. As-built sheets and a point cloud when the job starts from a scan. Typical drafting turnaround is 24–48 hours once the measure or scan is in. Retainer clients are scheduled ahead of one-off work.

Cities We Draw For

Eagle Mountain, Saratoga Springs, Lehi, American Fork, Herriman, South Jordan, Provo, and Orem, plus the rest of Utah County and Salt Lake County.
