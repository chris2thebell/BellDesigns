About

Chris Bell, drafter and Utah B100

I’m Chris Bell, a mechanical and quality engineer, a residential drafter, and a Utah general contractor. Bell Designs Utah is Licensed & Insured, Utah B100 [LICENSE #].

Engineering work taught me to check a drawing against the code and against what a crew can actually build. I use that same standard on basement finishes, remodels, additions, and ADUs: floor plans, electrical plans, and permit-ready sheets for homeowners and for general contractors.

Most drafting is back in 24–48 hours once I have measurements or a 3D scan. I’m based in Eagle Mountain and I draw projects in Saratoga Springs, Lehi, American Fork, Herriman, South Jordan, Provo, and Orem.

Send a sketch, a scan link, or a rough floor plan. I like talking through a layout before it goes on a sheet.
